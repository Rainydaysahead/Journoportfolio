Documentary

A short film on the women’s climbing sessions at BlocHaus in Openshaw, shot, directed, and edited solo.

This documentary was shot, directed, and edited by me on a Sony A7 III. I then used the Adobe suite to colour and correct the audio. The film looks at a new initiative BlocHaus Climbing introduced to include more women in their climbing gym in Openshaw.

The documentary follows a scheme the gym brought in to encourage more women to climb in their community for half the cost. Because of this project, I also got into bouldering, and I still climb there regularly.

It was a big project to take on solo, yet I found the scheme too important to leave unreported. Coordinating the camera, lighting, and tripod, and finding the right angles for the interviewees, was trial and error. It was a good challenge to my perseverance, and it showed the gym’s community support. This project refined my videography, editing, directing, and interviewing, while amplifying voices often overlooked in mainstream media.

The response went beyond the film: it prompted conversations, fostered mentorship among newcomers, and connected climbers across backgrounds. The entire experience was enjoyable and rewarding because I could see the idea come to life. It reinforced my commitment to storytelling that supports local communities. From each camera angle to each question, the documentary became an informative, entertaining piece that is still factual and important today.
