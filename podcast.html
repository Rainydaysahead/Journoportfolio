She Moves Manchester. A two-part series on entry-level women’s sport across the city.

Episode one

Episode two

Noticing a gap in women’s sport, I made a niche two-part podcast. It looks at entry-level sports across Manchester that are low-cost or free for women. The purpose is to encourage women to get active in their own communities at beginner level, without feeling judged.

Plenty of these sessions exist, and plenty of them pass unnoticed because they are smaller and less publicised. I wanted to give a voice to the smaller, not-for-profit organisations doing that work.

I sourced the interviewees and brought them onto She Moves Manchester. The interviews were edited in Adobe Audition and published on SoundCloud.
