Social Media

Brand work for a French patisserie: filming, editing, and posting across LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.

As a social media manager and content creator, I helped manage an up-and-coming French patisserie, building a brand image from LinkedIn to TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. I edited, filmed, and created content every week.

That let the brand show its newest delicacies: the fresh ingredients, the designs, and the food itself, without having to describe them line by line.

I worked with the owners so the content matched their standard, and helped shape a successful launch.
